Tidepool

Tidepool reads coastal tide gauges and predicts water depth at each berth, so small harbors can see when water will be too low for a scheduled ship.

Built for small ports that can't pay for a commercial tide service.
What it delivers

Depth forecasts per berth, built on the port's own gauge data

Gauge sync

Reads up to 40 gauges over the public NMEA feed. Gauges report every 6 minutes; every reading is stored in SQLite.

Tide prediction

A harmonic tide model per gauge, refit every night from the last 30 days of readings.

Low-water alerts (in progress)

Compares each ship's draft with predicted depth at its berth. Will text the harbor master 12 hours ahead once the SMS contract is signed; for now alerts go to a log file.

Port dashboard

One web page per port with the next 48 hours of predicted depth for each berth.

  • Public data only — uses the free NMEA gauge feed, no paid data source

  • Clear alert rule — flags a ship when under-keel margin is below 0.5 m
The headline feature

Tide predictions within 8 cm, 95% of the time

  • Measured at both pilot ports — prediction vs. observed water level at Port Calloway and Westhaven
  • Fitted per gauge — each gauge gets its own model, so local harbor effects are captured
  • Standard method — harmonic analysis over the five main tidal constituents (M2, S2, N2, K1, O1)
  • Self-updating — refit every night from the latest 30 days, no manual tuning

Why this matters

Every alert and every dashboard number rests on this prediction. With an 8 cm error band against a 0.5 m safety margin, the port gets depth forecasts it can plan around, without paying for a commercial tide service.
