For bookkeeping firms

ledgerline

Month-end bank reconciliation in minutes, not hours. ledgerline matches a client’s bank export against their invoice sheet and hands you only the payments that need a human.

Version 0.9 · used monthly by three bookkeeping firms since August 2025
What it delivers

Your team reviews the exceptions. ledgerline does the rest.

Reads your clients’ bank exports

CSV exports from Northbank, Harbor Credit, Fieldstone and Meridian, straight from online banking.

Takes the invoice sheet as it is

Invoice lists in CSV or Excel (XLSX). No re-keying into a new system.

Matches payments to invoices

Same amount, paid within 5 days of the due date, and a payer name that closely matches the customer.

Reports only what’s left

Unmatched payments and unpaid invoices, as a CSV and a one-page HTML summary you can send to the client.

How it works

One command per client, per month

Export
Download the bank CSV and the invoice sheet
→
Match
ledgerline pairs payments with invoices
→
Report
Exceptions written to CSV and HTML
→
Clear
Your bookkeeper resolves the short list
  • Clear rules — every match comes down to amount, date and name, so any match can be explained to a client or reviewer.
  • Forgiving on names — reordered names and extra words in the bank’s payer field still match.
  • Runs on your own computer — a command-line tool that reads local files. No account, no upload.
The result

From about 6 hours to 25 minutes

  • 1,240 payments — one test month of real reconciliation work.
  • 1,198 matched automatically — about 97% with no one touching them.
  • 42 left for review — cleared by a bookkeeper in 25 minutes.
  • ~6 hours by hand — what the same month took before ledgerline.

Why this matters

Reconciliation is the same work every month for every client. Cutting it from a day to under half an hour frees your bookkeepers for advisory work, or lets the same team take on more clients.
