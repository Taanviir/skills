What it does

Built around the files you already have

Reads four banks' exports

CSV exports from Northbank, Harbor Credit, Fieldstone and Meridian, as they come out of online banking.

Takes invoice sheets as-is

Invoice lists as CSV or Excel (XLSX). No re-keying into a new system.

Lists what doesn't match

Every payment without an invoice, and every invoice still unpaid, in one report.

Two report formats

A CSV to work through, and a one-page HTML summary to file or send to the client.

How it matches

Three checks, all of which must pass

  • Same amount — the payment equals the invoice total exactly.
  • Close in date — paid within 5 days of the invoice due date.
  • Same payer — the bank's payer name matches the customer name closely (85/100 or better), so "ACME LTD" still finds "Acme Ltd."
  • Otherwise, it's flagged — anything that fails a check goes on the review list rather than being guessed.

Why this matters

The rules are simple enough to explain to a client or an auditor. ledgerline only pairs a payment with an invoice when amount, date and name all agree. When it isn't sure, a person decides.
