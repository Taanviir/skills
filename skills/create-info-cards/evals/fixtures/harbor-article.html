Port Calloway reopens its rebuilt harbor after 19 months

By Mira Okafor, Calloway Ledger, 6 March 2026

The harbor at Port Calloway reopened to cargo ships on 4 March 2026, nineteen months after the town closed it for a full rebuild. The project cost $412 million, about $38 million less than the budget the town council approved in 2024.

The old harbor had two berths, both built in 1971. The rebuilt harbor has three, and each one is deep enough for ships drawing 14 metres of water. Before the rebuild, the deepest berth could take ships drawing only 9 metres, which kept most modern container ships away.

Six electric cranes replace the four diesel cranes that worked the old quay. Harbor master Tomas Lindqvist said the switch cuts the port's direct emissions by 38 percent. "The cranes were our biggest single source," he said. "Ships will also be able to plug into shore power at two of the three berths instead of running their engines while they unload."

The rebuild employed 1,850 people at its peak, most of them from Calloway County. The town expects the harbor to support 620 permanent jobs once it reaches full traffic, up from 410 before the closure.

Not everything went to plan. A storm in November 2025 flooded the new quay wall during construction and pushed the opening back by six weeks. The contractor, Rennick Marine, absorbed the repair cost under its contract, so the delay did not raise the price for the town.

Local fishing crews had worried that the rebuild would squeeze them out. The final design moved the fishing fleet to a separate basin on the north side with 48 berths, eight more than it had before. "We were braced for losing space, and we gained it," said Ana Sorell, who chairs the Calloway Fishermen's Association.

The port expects 140 cargo ship calls in its first year, rising to 300 a year by 2030. Mayor Delphine Achebe called the reopening "the most important day for this town's economy in fifty years" and said the council would publish monthly traffic figures so residents can track whether those numbers hold.
